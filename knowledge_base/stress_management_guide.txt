# Nexus AI Studio — Stress & Burnout Management Guide

## 1. Recognizing Stress & Academic/Work Burnout
Burnout is a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by excessive and prolonged stress. It occurs when you feel overwhelmed, emotionally drained, and unable to meet constant demands.

### Key Signs:
- Chronic fatigue and exhaustion
- Cynicism, detachment, or feeling isolated
- Lack of accomplishment or reduced productivity
- Physical symptoms like headaches, muscle tension, or disrupted sleep patterns

## 2. The 4-7-8 Mindful Breathing Technique
When anxiety or panic spikes, the 4-7-8 breathing method helps activate your parasympathetic nervous system:
1. Exhale completely through your mouth, making a whoosh sound.
2. Close your mouth and inhale quietly through your nose to a mental count of 4.
3. Hold your breath for a count of 7.
4. Exhale completely through your mouth, making a whoosh sound to a count of 8.
5. Repeat this cycle 4 times.

## 3. The 2-Minute Reset Protocol
If studying or working feels paralyzing:
- Step 1: Physical Disconnect: Step away from all screens for 2 full minutes.
- Step 2: Sensory Grounding (5-4-3-2-1): Notice 5 things you can see, 4 you can physically feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 positive affirmation about yourself.
- Step 3: Micro-Tasking: Pick only ONE tiny task (e.g. read 1 paragraph or write 1 sentence). Complete only that task.

## 4. Setting Healthy Boundaries
- The Power of Saying No: Politely decline tasks that exceed your realistic capacity.
- Sleep Protection: Avoid bright blue-light screens at least 45 minutes before sleep.
- Nutrition & Hydration: Drink a glass of water before starting high-focus sessions.
